# Printer Troubleshooting – Acme Corp

All office printers use **PaperCut** secure print. Jobs are held until you tap your badge at any printer.

## Adding a printer
- Windows: Settings > Printers > Add > select "Acme-FollowMe-Print".
- Mac: install the PaperCut client from Company Portal first.

## Job not releasing
1. Check the PaperCut balance widget – jobs over 50 pages need manager approval.
2. Held jobs are deleted after **4 hours**.
3. If the badge is not recognised, re-register it at the printer: tap badge > enter employee ID.

Printer hardware faults (paper jams, toner) go to Facilities, not IT: facilities@acme-internal.com.
