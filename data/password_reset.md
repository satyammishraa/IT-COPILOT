# Password Reset Policy – Acme Corp

- Passwords expire every **90 days**.
- Minimum 14 characters, must include a number and a symbol. The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.

## Self-service reset
1. Go to **passwordreset.acme-internal.com**
2. Enter your employee ID (format: ACM-12345).
3. Verify with Microsoft Authenticator or your registered mobile number.

## Locked out?
After 5 failed attempts the account locks for **30 minutes**. For an immediate unlock, call the IT Service Desk at extension **4357** (H-E-L-P) or raise a P2 ticket in ServiceNow.
