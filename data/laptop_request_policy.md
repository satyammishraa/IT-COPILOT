# Laptop Request & Refresh Policy – Acme Corp

- Standard laptop: **Dell Latitude 7450** (Windows 11) for most roles.
- Engineering and Data teams may request a **MacBook Pro 14"** with manager approval.
- Laptops are refreshed every **4 years**.

## How to request
Raise a "Hardware Request" in ServiceNow > IT Catalog. Approval flow: Manager → IT Asset Team. Delivery SLA is **5 business days** in Bengaluru and Pune, 10 elsewhere.

## Lost or stolen laptop
Report within 24 hours via ServiceNow (P1) and to security@acme-internal.com. The device will be remotely wiped through Intune.
